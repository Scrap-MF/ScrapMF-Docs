# 📥 Download content

**Download content** is the first entry of the main menu. It runs scrapes
against the accounts or URLs you choose and writes the results under your
output directory.

From here you can take one of three paths:

- **[Saved profile](./saved-profile)** — reuse a saved profile and pick the
  accounts and content kinds it covers.
- **[URL(s)](./urls)** — paste one or more URLs; the site is auto-detected
  per URL.
- **[Quick scrape](./quick-scrape)** — flatten the directory tree to
  `<account>/<content>`.
- **[Tasks](./tasks)** — saved content batches: content chosen once, reused.

All paths share the same dashboard, integrity report and persistent log at
the end of the run. See [site-specific flows](./site-flows) for behaviour
that differs by network (Twitter/X two-pass, Instagram posts/reels, …).
